Privacy Policy

Bubblop · Effective date: October 22, 2026

Bubblop is a puzzle game. We built it so that it does not need any of your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things only on your device so it works the way you left it:

  • your best score and the number of games played
  • your sound, vibration and ball-shape settings
  • whether you have completed the tutorial

The game never sends this information anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If you use your phone's own backup (Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud backup), that backup may include this small file, under your control; we never receive it.

Internet access

The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's store listing will say so.
