# Privacy Policy

**Bubblop** · Effective date: October 16, 2026

Bubblop is a puzzle game. We built it so that it does not need any of your personal
information.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. It
has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
The game saves a few things **only on your device** so it works the way you left it:
- your best score and the number of games played
- your sound, vibration and ball-shape settings
- whether you have completed the tutorial

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Internet access
The game works fully offline and does not send data over the internet.

## Children
The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads), this policy
will be updated before that version is released, and the app's store listing will say so.
