## Full description (4000)
Drop. Match. Chain!

Bubblop is a bright, satisfying puzzle game you can play with one thumb. Drop balls
into the board, connect 4 of the same colour to pop them, and watch the board come
alive with confetti.

**Chain reactions are the real prize.** When balls pop, everything above falls, and if
that makes a new match, it pops too. Every step of a chain scores more than the last and
plays the next note of a happy little tune.

**Bubbles are your secret weapon.** Rows of bubbles rise from below. Pop balls next to a
bubble to break it open and free the ball inside, which can set off an even bigger chain.

★ Learn in seconds, master for months
★ One-thumb play: drag to aim, release to drop
★ Big, juicy chain reactions with sound and vibration
★ Beat your best score: every round takes just a few minutes
★ Colour-blind friendly: optional shapes on every ball
★ Plays offline · No account needed · No data collected
