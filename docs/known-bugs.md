# Known bugs and limitations

## Open
- **Unexplained once (2026-10-16):** on Windows, `tutorialDone` was found saved as true
  although no tutorial had been finished there. It could not be reproduced from a clean
  state (the tutorial starts and the home screen blocks taps correctly). Watch for it.
- **Sounds not yet heard by a person.** They are generated by `tools/make_sfx.py`;
  volumes and tones may need tuning after a listen.
- **Rare frame spikes**: at most 1 per 60-second benchmark (up to 41 ms). Cause not
  identified (possibly shader warm-up or garbage collection).
- **Performance not measured on a phone.** The numbers below are from the Windows PC's
  integrated GPU. The emulator only runs debug builds, which don't give meaningful timings.
- The upper part of the board is empty early in a round.
- **Web build:** the ball shader draws misplaced in the browser, so the web version uses the
  simpler Canvas ball material (`OrbSprites.loadProgram`). No vibration on the web.
- On short or wide screens the board is limited by height and gets side margins.

## Fixed (2026-10-22)
- **Taps sometimes did nothing:** a press held longer than ~0.1 s without moving dropped
  no ball (touch and mouse). On release the gesture arena cancels the unused drag, which
  cleared the aim just before the tap's "up" arrived. A tap now drops where it lifted.
  Checked across touch/mouse Г— 8 movement distances Г— 5 hold times, and in the real app.
- **Tutorial could stall:** only the first ball was scripted blue, so after a miss the
  "try dropping it here" hint pointed at a column where a random colour might not pop.
  The first three balls are now blue (test/tutorial_test.dart).
- The landing ring could float above settling balls when tapping mid-chain (it follows
  the rules board, which is a move ahead); it now shows only once the board is still.
- Colour-blind option: **Ball shapes** in the pause menu puts a white triangle, star,
  square, plus or ring on each colour (off by default; saved).
- Very long scores now shrink to fit the score panel instead of overflowing.
- Android back during a round closed the app; it now pauses (back again resumes, and
  from a finished round it goes home). Escape does the same on PC and web.

## Fixed (2026-10-16)
- The "Bubblop" logo overflowed its box by 108 px on narrow screens; it now scales to fit.
- The "How to play" button label overflowed in the pause menu; button labels now shrink to fit.

## Fixed (2026-10-15)
- Bright redesign: the board card was drawn at 33% opacity because the shadow's paint
  alpha faded the card's gradient. The colour is now reset before shader fills.
- Old step labels overlapped new ones in chains: they now clear almost immediately.
- HUD ball icons drawn as vector graphics cost ~8 ms of raster time per frame (dark
  design); they use the baked sprites.
- After aiming once, board fills faded out (shared paint alpha); solid and shader paints
  are now separate.
- A pre-filled board (chain demo) was invisible until balls moved.
- A 3-colour gradient without stops threw during paint.

## Limitations
- iOS release builds need cloud CI (Codemagic) or a Mac; see docs/MOBILE_RELEASE_GUIDE.md.
